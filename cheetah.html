Cheetah Protection

~ the fastest cats, and how to help save them ~

0 · Ultimate goal

Cheetahs are running out of room, and the help they need is deadly urgent. I'm not a biologist, I own no land in Africa, and I write no policy — so my contribution has to be built out of what an ordinary person actually has.

The ultimate goal: make my ordinary life quietly load-bearing for this species — a stream of money the people on the ground can count on, an understanding deep enough that the money goes to the right place, and enough said out loud that a few other ordinary people join in.
